# Matching, priority and report

Match on corroborating evidence, in this order: exact stable matter/file
identifier; recorded participant address plus compatible subject matter;
distinctive party/property/transaction/court reference plus a recorded name;
or verified thread continuity. A name, sender, domain or fuzzy similarity alone
does not establish a match. Search relevant register fields, including open and
closed matters, before calling an item unidentified. A court number absent from
the register warrants a separate party-based search, not an invented match.
Newly opened matters may postdate the snapshot; disclose that possibility.

Record high-confidence, tentative or unresolved identification and its basis.
Only a high-confidence match supports a proposed matter-specific change without
an identification question. One thread may concern several matters.

Use four attention bands:

- **Protective:** plausible limitation/court exposure, emergency, immediate
  funds risk or another irreversible consequence; preserve uncertainty.
- **Today:** a supported near-term commitment, material client instruction,
  settlement expiry, court/counsel issue or blocked transaction.
- **Plan:** actionable work with no established immediate consequence.
- **Awareness:** relevant information with no identified action.

Tone, repetition and an "urgent" label are signals, not priority conclusions.

Present, in order: scope and timestamp; protective/today items; other actionable
matters; tracker alerts; items needing identification; exact proposed changes;
coverage and unresolved questions. Each significant item names the matter,
source locator, event/request, earliest plausible consequence, date status,
owner and recommended action. Show competing dates and sources rather than
silently choosing one.

Proposed changes use one row per operation:

`system | matter | field/event | old value | proposed value | source | uncertainty`

Calendar proposals include exact event identity, timezone and intended effects
under the owning workflow. Report pages not traversed, unreviewed attachments,
caps, failed sources and stale evidence. Do not bury protective items in counts.

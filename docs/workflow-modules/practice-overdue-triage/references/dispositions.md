# Evidence and dispositions

For each candidate, retain the exact matter identity, current canonical value
and revision, category, governing source/date status, targeted evidence,
evidence against the proposed conclusion, earliest plausible consequence and
proposed disposition. Silence or elapsed time cannot establish completion.

Use these dispositions:

- **Keep and escalate:** preserve the entry; propose a specific protective next
  action if supported.
- **Reschedule next action:** a supported new action/date with an approved
  historical record of the old item and reason.
- **Record completion:** reliable completion evidence, a sourced timeline entry
  and an appropriate next action; preserve history.
- **Resolve or replace court date:** source-backed order/compliance/supersession
  plus exact lawyer approval through the tracker workflow.
- **Limitation protective review:** preserve and escalate until the lawyer
  resolves the verified legal inputs and expressly approves any change.
- **Closure candidate:** route to the owning close workflow with separate clear
  approval, not a generic cleanup operation.
- **Unknown, lawyer decision required:** no mutation.

The proposal table has one row per exact operation:

`matter | category | old canonical value | disposition | new value | evidence | uncertainty | calendar effect`

List clearings/deletions explicitly. Keep unresolved limitations, unproven court
compliance, missing/malformed protective data, conflicting status evidence and
source gaps in the leading report even if no writes are proposed. Identify the
protective action, owner and decision required. A completed mutation does not
resolve an evidentiary gap.

# Proposition ledgers

Use a working ledger proportionate to the requested submission. It may be a
compact table for a short section. Its purpose is to make each material claim
auditable, not to impose a new tracker schema or automatically save matter memory.

## Fact ledger

| Exact proposition | Record source/version | Stable locator | Status | Qualification or gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claim intended for the draft | Document, message, transcript, order or supplied account | Page, paragraph, exhibit/tab, timestamp or stable message ID | verified / inferred / disputed / missing | Contrary evidence or decision needed |

`Verified` means the cited source was read and supports the claim at the stated
strength. It does not certify the underlying event as uncontested. Attribute an
allegation or witness/client account. Put an inference's reasoning and contrary
evidence in the working notes; obtain a lawyer decision when it materially
affects the submission. Missing support cannot become an unqualified assertion.

## Authority ledger

| Exact proposition | Primary authority | Pinpoint | Verification performed | Currency/treatment limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rule or legal test used | Full-text decision, statute, rule or direction | Paragraph, section or rule | Text and surrounding context read; identifiers checked | Date/current consolidation, subsequent history and contrary authority checked |

Secondary commentary, snippets, headnotes and recalled authorities are discovery
aids. Verify the proposition in primary text before relying on it. Record any
access failure and narrow or omit the affected claim. Do not treat absence of a
located negative result as proof an authority is good law.

## Final sentence check

Compare every material final sentence against its ledger row. Re-check quotations
and surrounding qualifications, and ensure the source actually supports the
precise application rather than a neighbouring proposition. Verify each source
locator remains meaningful in the delivered version.

Keep factual assertions, legal rules and advocacy in distinct roles. Include
adverse material that changes the result. Working uncertainty labels remain
until resolved or the proposition is removed; the completion notes identify
the remaining gaps without implying clearance.

# Sources and propositions

Use this for substantive advice, document comparison, research, advocacy and
evidence summaries. Start with the current question, controlling instrument and
current instructions. Consult previous advice to recover an instruction or find
a source, not to preserve a conclusion contradicted by the primary record.

Build a compact proposition ledger when the task has several material claims:

| Proposition | Kind | Source and stable locator | Status | Gap or contrary evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What the final text needs to establish | Fact, allegation, inference or legal submission | Document page/paragraph, exact message ID, primary authority pinpoint, or attributed user instruction | Verified, inferred, disputed or missing | What remains unresolved |

This is a human-reviewed working table, not a new machine schema or automatic
finding of truth. Distinguish what a document shows, what a party alleges, what
the lawyer instructed and what the argument infers. Preserve conflicting dates
or versions until a controlling source resolves them. Multiple copies of one
document or quoted email history are not independent corroboration.

When evidence identifies a newly material person, entity or property whose role
may involve another firm client or adverse interest, use the installed tracker's
bounded read-only open/closed candidate search. Reuse a still-current documented
search rather than repeating it for every mention. Report minimal matching
metadata and the relevance to the present task; do not open another client's
contents or infer clearance from a missing hit. A potential adverse position or
use of another file's confidential knowledge goes to the lawyer under
[intake/conflict](intake-and-lifecycle.md). This is a targeted relationship check,
not an automatic whole-practice audit.

Read every source needed for a material claim through the installed extraction
routes. Document presence, filename, extracted text or a signature image does
not establish execution, transmission, receipt, filing or service. Inspect the
actual source evidence needed for that status. Disclose unread pages, missing
attachments, source/version gaps and retrieval limits beside affected claims.
Follow the [correspondence completeness contract](correspondence-completeness.md)
when quotations, inline material or attached messages affect the proposition.

Research each relied-on legal proposition against current primary text. Verify
the precise passage, citation, court/jurisdiction, pinpoint, effective or
consolidation date and any controlling order/practice direction. For case law,
check available subsequent history and adverse treatment; inability to complete
that check is a disclosed research gap. Search results, summaries and recalled
law are discovery aids. If no authorized research route or full text is
available, narrow the proposition or state the limitation; do not manufacture
verification. The workflow does not supply a research connector or legal advice.

Before delivery, compare each material final sentence to its source. Recheck
figures, units, currency, thresholds, names, roles, dates, quotations, commitments,
defined terms and requested relief. Re-derive a comparison or net effect, rather
than merely tracing its inputs. Read the strongest material contrary source and
explain the supported resolution or remaining uncertainty. An absence claim is
no broader than the corpus actually reviewed.

Apply [date classification](dates.md) and [client-advice review](client-advice.md)
where relevant. Keep privileged strategy/source notes outside circulating
artifacts. Internal advocacy drafts may retain explicit source-gap labels under
their owning module; correspondence and circulating copies may not silently
carry drafting markers. Label the artifact and its actual readiness accurately.

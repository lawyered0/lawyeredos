# Verify redline meaning

Use this reference after the substantive positions and exact edits are
established. Contract review decides those positions; the redline skill
implements and verifies them through its existing declared engine and writers.
This reference grants no negotiation authority and adds no command or engine
feature.

## Establish the intended effect

Identify the authoritative source version, the approved position, and the
exact change for each affected provision. Preserve the source's existing review
state; do not silently accept another reviewer's revisions or reconstruct a
contract from a PDF. When a requested change still requires a strategic choice,
state that decision and complete only the established independent edits.

Keep internal reasons, fallback terms, and walkaway positions outside the
counterparty artifact. Alternative positions can be separate identified
proposals when requested, but a circulating clause must have one coherent
operative meaning. Do not combine incompatible alternatives, leave a choice
inside operative wording, or treat an internal question as agreed text.

## Read the change in its instrument

Check each marked edit and the intended accepted text against surrounding
clauses and the approved position. Trace the actual effect on:

- the actor, duty, right, exception, condition, and consequence;
- timing, notice, cure, renewal, termination, and survival;
- amounts, definitions, thresholds, exclusions, and dependencies; and
- numbering, defined terms, references, schedules, party names, and execution
  provisions affected by the change.

A unique text match and successful insertion/deletion do not establish that
the edited contract says what the lawyer intended. Re-derive any calculation
or comparison needed for the stated effect under source-verification and
dates; do not use a prior review's conclusion as primary evidence. Check for
qualifications lost by deletion, exceptions stranded by a replacement, and
unchanged provisions that defeat or expand the requested effect.

Do not invent a new concession to reconcile an inconsistency. If the intended
effect needs a further decision, identify the affected provisions and the
consequence. Revise only within established authority and recheck materially
changed substance.

## Verify the actual requested outputs

Follow all existing redline skill checks: successful full application, genuine
Word revisions and configured author, intended accepted text, source stability,
package/content inspection, and complete-page visual QA. Color or strikeout
formatting alone is not a genuine tracked revision. Confirm both deleted and
inserted wording are legible and that numbering, tables, headers/footers,
signatures, and page breaks still work in the marked and clean renderings.

Replacement and deletion preserve each affected run's formatting; inserted
paragraphs track both their text and paragraph marks, without inheriting the
anchor's section break or paragraph-property revisions. The engine conservatively
refuses spans whose runs contain page, column, or rendered-page-break markers.
Use a narrower span in another run or report that layout requires manual review;
do not flatten or remove a break to make the operation succeed.

Compare the source rendering with the marked output as well as marked with clean.
Inspect visible revision markup, orphan headings/numbering and unchanged
formatting where edits can affect meaning. Verify a change-set schedule's clause
label against the provision's actual placement; a matching label alone does not
prove the right clause changed. Do not expand an approved substantive edit to
repair a newly discovered issue without a supported choice.

Compare the clean text with the intended accepted marked text across the body
and relevant headers, footers, notes and other substantive parts through the
declared extraction route. A body-only comparison cannot clear an ancillary-part
gap. Keep any unverified part or unexpected acceptance effect visible.

Keep verification copies in private scratch unless their delivery or save was
requested. Persist only requested, verified versions through the declared
guarded writer. A clean-copy check must not flatten an unsupported revision
class, unresolved comment, or failed extraction to obtain a success label.
The clean-copy helper refuses paragraph-mark and table-row deletions because
accepting them requires a structural merge or removal it does not implement.
Resolve those revisions manually in a reviewed copy; it leaves the source intact
and produces no clean output for those cases.
If a requested format cannot be verified with the available route, preserve
the supported work and report that format's exact limitation.

Before circulation, inspect for unintended concessions, privileged notes,
drafting comments, placeholder text, residual revisions in a purported clean
copy, and unintended metadata. Apply the owning review policy where required.
Record source version, established changes, requested output paths and status,
review coverage, and unresolved decisions under records-and-handoffs. Saving
or review never proves counterparty acceptance, execution, or delivery.

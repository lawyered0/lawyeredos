# Correspondence completeness

Use this reference when a reply depends on difficult email content or when
preparing the final form of a letter or notice. It adds reading and completion
checks; it supplies no extraction tool, sending authority, or new writer option.
The client-advice contract owns retainer-ending preparation, owner source
checks, required review, and complete or revised email delivery. The dates
contract owns date classes, calculations, and proposed response windows.

## Read the actual communication

A snippet, attachment count, filename, or earlier brief is a locator. Read the
current message and the controlling material before stating what was said,
provided, requested, or agreed. Identify whose words each passage contains.
Quoted history is not automatically a new instruction from its forwarder.

- **Inline visual material:** a screenshot, annotated image, or embedded visual
  can carry the substantive instruction even when no ordinary attachment is
  listed. Use a separately available authorized read route to obtain its actual
  content, with source and coverage recorded. If that route is unavailable,
  request the relevant text and leave dependent advice unresolved. A signature
  image or logo does not by itself require a substantive review.
- **Replies inside quoted text:** read each answer with its question. Formatting
  may distinguish a new answer from the quoted original; plain text may erase
  that distinction. Do not attribute ambiguous text to the current sender.
  Obtain a complete readable rendering or identified answers before relying on
  the affected passage.
- **Attached or forwarded email:** read its own headers, body, and material
  attachments through an actually supported route. The enclosing message's
  body and attachment metadata do not substitute for them. An unsupported or
  unreadable message remains a named coverage gap; do not infer it was never
  sent or received.

Keep acquired material within the owning workflow's existing source and save
rules. No new parser or fallback route is authorized here. A separate reading
does not permit acknowledging an incomplete machine review chunk as complete.
Report exactly what remains unread, stop only work dependent on it, and finish
independent supported work.

## Confirm the final letter or notice

Check the circulating version against the requested purpose and primary sources:

1. The addressee, organization, representative, address, subject, and matter or
   proceeding identifier match verified evidence. Intended audience and actual
   transmission audience are different facts; use the existing audience
   reconciliation route when current correspondence controls the audience.
2. The letter carries the intended explicit static date. Distinguish that
   document date from a legal trigger, asserted deadline, or proposed response
   window. Do not assume creation, delivery, service, receipt, or execution
   occurred on the printed date.
   Check the document's date representation through available structural tools:
   its intended date must be static text rather than a field that updates on
   opening. If that representation cannot be verified, disclose the limitation
   before calling a time-sensitive copy ready for circulation.
3. Each enclosure or attachment mentioned is identified by its exact verified
   version and is actually available. A file list alone does not establish its
   contents or that it accompanied an earlier transmission. State an unresolved
   attachment dependency outside copy-ready text as the client-advice contract
   requires; do not describe absent material as enclosed.
4. Any statement about signing, execution, filing, service, payment, or delivery
   is supported by evidence of that event. Extracted text and a filename do not
   prove a signature; a draft and a sender address do not prove transmission.
   Keep unresolved event state explicit.
5. The requested position, response, or notice is complete within the established
   authority. The relevant specialized source module supplies substantive notice
   conditions, remedies, or professional requirements. Resolve a necessary
   choice rather than hiding alternatives inside the operative communication.
6. The external copy is free of privileged rationale, drafting notes, unwanted
   comments or revisions, placeholder text, and unintended metadata. Inspect
   the actual requested DOCX/PDF pages and package parts through declared tools;
   rendering and text inspection answer different questions.

Use the existing letterhead workflow for generation, lint, rendering, final
save, and review. Record the artifact's actual status and any remaining
dependency under records-and-handoffs. A verified internal draft does not
authorize or prove sending, signing, filing, or service.

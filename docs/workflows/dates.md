# Date classification and promotion

Report a material date with source, class and verification status. Do not turn
an extracted event, party demand or lawyer response request into a legal deadline.

| Class | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Asserted event or demand | Attribute it to the party/document; assertion does not make it binding. Preserve conflicting dates. |
| Legal/procedural/statutory | Identify current governing law/order, jurisdiction and lawyer-confirmed trigger/inputs. This package supplies no legal-deadline calculator; do not compute one with generic arithmetic or from memory. |
| Contractual notice, cure, renewal or payment | Read and quote the controlling clause and identify required inputs. Until the lawyer confirms the clause and inputs, report them rather than a computed candidate date. Use an expressly supplied verified calculation or separately authorized appropriate facility; no contractual calculator is added. |
| Specialized regime | Identify any potentially applicable specialist regime and its verification need; do not apply a generic civil rule to it. |
| Strategic response/follow-up window | Disclose proposed interval, anchor and resulting proposed date when supported. Label it a lawyer-selected request, without inventing consequences for missing it. |

Use statuses `asserted`, `proposed`, `calculated-for-verification` and
`lawyer-confirmed` as prose labels; these are not added tracker fields.
`Calculated-for-verification` describes a calculation actually supplied or
performed through a separately authorized suitable facility, never one inferred
from this instruction. Confirmation of a date is distinct from approval to write
it to the register or Calendar.

Before external handoff of a time-sensitive draft, recheck its static date,
source version and any anchor for a relative window. Recalculate/reconfirm only
through the appropriate authorized route when inputs changed. Do not let a
stale draft silently alter a period. A proposed window included in an internal
draft still requires lawyer review of the rendered content.

Promote, alter or retire dates only through the existing tracker/calendar
approval procedures. A passed date, silence or closed-looking folder is not
completion evidence. Preserve unresolved protective items and identify the
source or decision needed. No specialist calculation or automatic legal
verification is created by the Ontario boundary profile.

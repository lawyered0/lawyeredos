# Client advice, review and delivery

Load this for a client reply, advice email, covering message or communication
changing the retainer. Use the [source contract](source-verification.md) and
[scope contract](scope-and-authority.md); a draft remains for lawyer review.

## Before changing a retainer

Before drafting an ending, narrowing, referral or refusal of further work, read
the outstanding written commitments, current client instructions, known
availability/incapacity/absence, and the governing source and class of every
proposed date. Surface a conflict between instructions and an existing commitment
to the lawyer before choosing wording. Do not invent a withdrawal duty or deadline;
applicable professional rules and protective steps require current verification
and lawyer judgment. A final review cannot replace this preparation.

## The owner's check

Answer every material question. Distinguish a recommendation or another party's
proposal from consent already given by the lawyer or client. Do not assume
willingness to settle, accept terms, attend, extend, waive or make a commitment.
Place substantive uncertainty in the advice; put lawyer-only confirmations
beside the affected draft, outside its copy-ready body.

Check the final text against primary sources, current instructions, audience and
authority. Record an internal `Outbound check (date)` paragraph identifying the
actual traced sources, unsupported items and how they were removed, qualified
or retained as explicit uncertainty. This is a host-written review record, not
a new enforced writer flag. Keep it outside external content; a generic claim
that a check occurred is insufficient.
Keep the paragraph in the completion response or private scratch unless a
matter-record save is expressly approved or it belongs to the requested internal
draft. It does not independently authorize a record, tracker or account write.

## Proportionate independent review

Use one independent same-provider fact/source reviewer for substantive advice
exceeding the practice's recorded review threshold, or wording the client will
send to a third party. Without a recorded threshold, use source tracing when
the advice makes consequential recommendations or combines material factual
and legal propositions that need an independent support check. Do not add a
reviewer to a short routine factual acknowledgment for reassurance. Record any
practice threshold in an approved practice note, not an invented configuration
key. Brief the reviewer
with the exact body and extracted cited source text. Review factual support,
dates, names and commitments; do not add strategy/style lenses for reassurance.

A retained written opinion over a lawyer's signature takes the installed full
panel when available. Preserve the existing correspondence skill's consequential
position triggers and any explicit user-requested review scope. An already
required panel can include source tracing without a duplicate reviewer. Use
only the active host's independent delegation and existing panel contracts.
Missing independent delegation makes the required review unavailable, not
equivalent to a main-thread check. Retain the internal work, report the review
gap and withhold the copy-ready body while required review is pending.

Verify findings against sources and correct accepted material findings. Keep
reviewed versions, gaps and dispositions under [records and handoffs](records-and-handoffs.md).
Reopen affected substantive checks after a material change; do not assert
clearance solely from stable hashes or a completed reviewer count.

## Deliver the cleared version

Provide one complete email with intended From, To, Cc and Subject followed by
the full body, in the host's normal copyable formatting. On a requested revision,
deliver the complete new version once. A saved path is not email delivery.
Identify requested attachments by verified saved location or retrieved source;
never invent an attachment path or imply an unavailable file is attached.

Use retrieved current headers through the existing audience reconciliation route
when the draft depends on them. Warn the lawyer explicitly if the proposed reply
omits an incoming Cc recipient, and distinguish deliberate omission from missing
header evidence. Verification supplies evidence, not sending authority. Retain
source/review notes separately. Report actual inline delivery or saved-draft
state and any unresolved decision; neither means sent, received, filed or served.

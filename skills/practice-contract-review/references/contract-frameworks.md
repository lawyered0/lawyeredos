# Contract frameworks

Load the framework appropriate to the document and the client's confirmed side.
These are issue prompts, not fixed sign-off thresholds. Derive preferred terms
from the deal and instructions; verify applicable law for legal conclusions.

## Confidentiality agreements

Check the definition and identification of protected information; public,
previously known, independently developed and properly received information;
permitted purpose and recipients; responsibility for representatives; legally
required disclosure and notice; term and survival; trade-secret treatment;
return/deletion, backups and regulatory retention; residual knowledge; remedies,
indemnity and liability; and governing law/forum.

Identify embedded non-compete, non-solicitation, exclusivity, IP transfer or
standstill provisions as separate commercial restrictions. Explain their effect
rather than treating them as ordinary confidentiality terms. A traffic-light
rating may summarize the review, but neither a label nor a familiar term authorizes
signature.

## Services, vendor agreements and statements of work

Prioritize liability caps and carve-outs; indemnity scope and procedure; ownership
of deliverables and background IP; licence rights; term, termination and cure;
privacy/data obligations; insurance; forum and dispute process; assignment and
change of control; and force majeure.

Also verify scope, dependencies, acceptance, change control, fees/taxes, expenses,
payment timing, suspension rights, warranties, subcontracting and post-termination
transition. Reconcile each statement of work with the master agreement and its
order-of-precedence clause. Check whether exclusions, indemnities or other
remedies defeat the apparent liability cap.

## SaaS, cloud and subscription overlay

Add renewal and cancellation mechanics, price changes, service levels and credits,
availability exclusions, security commitments, incident response, subprocessors,
data use, portability/export, return/deletion and transition access. Examine
incorporated online policies and unilateral update rights. Determine the applicable
privacy regime and source the relevant requirements before asserting a violation.

## Leases

Identify commercial or residential scope and the applicable regime. Review base
and additional rent, tax/insurance/common-area allocations, escalation, term,
renewal and notice, permitted use/exclusivity, possession and condition,
maintenance/repair/capital costs, alterations, assignment/subletting, guarantees,
default, termination, remedies and end-of-term obligations.

Check notice mechanisms and conditions on renewal or assignment. Distinguish the
contractual allocation from any mandatory statutory rule. Do not import a
commercial-lease position into a residential agreement or assume one locality's
tenancy law applies elsewhere.

## Employment and independent-contractor arrangements

Verify the governing jurisdiction and the intended relationship. Review role,
compensation/bonus/equity, benefits, expenses, confidentiality, IP and prior
inventions, restrictive covenants, termination/notice, benefits continuation,
release conditions and dispute resolution. Examine linked policies and discretion
clauses affecting compensation.

Source current statutory minimums and relevant termination/restrictive-covenant
authority before advising on enforceability. Classification and enforceability
require facts and applicable law; a contract label alone settles neither.

## Other transactions and overlays

For acquisitions, financing, licensing or another transaction, identify the
consideration, conditions, representations, covenants, risk allocation, completion
mechanics and post-completion obligations relevant to that deal. Explicitly scope
specialist issues requiring additional expertise or sources. Apply a privacy,
regulated-sector or other overlay only when the facts engage it.

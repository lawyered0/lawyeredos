# Chronology output

Use a proportionate format for the requested work. A canonical working chronology
records matter identity, version/date, source coverage, privilege posture and
instructed framing before the entries. Mark it internal work product as appropriate
to the practice; a label alone does not establish legal privilege.

## Entries

| Date or supported interval | Event or attributed position | Significance | Privilege/use flag | Sources and locators |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| As shown by evidence | No stronger than the supporting source | key / relevant / background | usable / potentially privileged / uncertain | Retrievable file/version, message ID or supplied instruction, with locator |

For a supplied instruction with no independent record, identify the speaker and
task/conversation source and attribute it as that person's account. A source
missing from the collection goes to gaps rather than an unsupported event row.

Add concise detail for key events when it explains why they support or undermine
the instructed position. Label theory connections and allegations. Retain both
versions of materially conflicting evidence and cross-reference their sources.

## Coverage and gaps

Record the actual source set, material date ranges not covered, missing evidence
categories, unreadable sources, retrieval/extraction caps, unresolved conflicts
and privilege decisions. Distinguish an empty searched interval from a claim that
nothing happened. Preserve prior limits when updating until they are resolved.

A requested saved chronology uses clean explanatory prose for uncertainty rather
than unresolved template directions. Scan every entry for source attribution and
unintended drafting markers before delivery. This is separate from whether an
event's evidentiary conflict has been resolved.

## Variants

- **Statement of facts:** use key and relevant entries to build a sourced
  narrative; attribute disputes and allegations. Exclude potentially privileged
  and uncertain-use material unless the lawyer specifically approves its use.
- **Witness-specific timeline:** filter events by the person's supported role
  as sender, recipient, attendee, actor or subject. Do not infer firsthand
  knowledge from being mentioned or copied on a message.
- **Issue-specific timeline:** retain events bearing on the instructed issue
  and state the filter/coverage limits so excluded background is not mistaken
  for nonexistent evidence.

Every variant retains source locators and its privilege/coverage boundary. A
filtered or exported version does not change the canonical internal record or
establish readiness for disclosure, filing or service.
